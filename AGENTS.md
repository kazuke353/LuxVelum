# AGENTS.md

Luxvelum ("Atmospheric Immersion for Project Reforged — WoW 3.3.5") is a fork of CoAVolFog, a Win32 Direct3D 9
volumetric fog and water DLL for the Ascension/CoA WoW 3.3.5a (12340) client. `Wow.exe`, patched with the Luxvelum
loader of St0ny's AIO WoW EXE Patcher, or else the optional `version.dll` proxy, loads `Luxvelum.dll`, which wraps
Direct3D 9, retargets a few engine call sites, reshades the client's water after its liquid pass and draws the fog after
the world. It is built with CMake and MSVC; the shaders are compiled with `fxc`. `README.md` holds the design and the
reverse-engineering evidence.

## Agent rules

- Configure once with `cmake -S . -B build -G "Visual Studio 17 2022" -A Win32`, build with
  `cmake --build build --config Release`, test with `ctest --test-dir build -C Release --output-on-failure`
  (the D3D9 harness and the comment check).
- Formatting follows `.editorconfig`: UTF-8, max 120 cols, trailing newline, no trailing whitespace, 4-space indent
  (tabs forbidden).
- Express intent through names, structure and tests; do not add explanatory comments or docstrings in C++, HLSL,
  Python or CMake. Name engine addresses, offsets and constants after what they are, and split code into named
  functions instead of commenting blocks. Preserve legal notices and tool directives. `python tools/check_comments.py`
  enforces this. The comments in `Luxvelum.ini` are the players' settings documentation and stay.
- Client addresses target the 12340 image (timestamp `0x4C2452FE`). Verify the bytes of a call site before patching
  it, and record the disassembly evidence in `README.md`, not in code.
- Add a harness check in `tests/harness.cpp` for a meaningful behaviour change; a regression should fail before the
  fix and pass after it. Harness checks do not establish in-game behaviour; say so when reporting.
- `data/fogdata.bin` is generated by `tools/convert_classic_fog.py` from the WoW Forever fog and lighting kit (folder
  or zip), placed by the `Light` and `ZoneLight` records of the previous `fogdata.bin` (or of the Classic client's CSV
  exports), which the kit could not decrypt, and then by `tools/convert_client_lights.py`, which adds the maps Classic
  never had from the 3.3.5 client's `DBFilesClient` light tables; regenerate it rather than editing it.
  `data/fogdata-without-darker-nights.bin` is made the same way from the `LightIntBand` without Project Reforged's
  darker nights patch (patch-N); `data/fogdata.bin` takes the one with it.
  `data/waterdata.bin` is generated by `tools/convert_forever_water.py` from the WoW Forever water kit (folder or zip)
  in the same way.
- The shipped `Luxvelum.ini` always carries the owner's settings: whenever you install into his client or prepare a
  release, compare his `WTF\Luxvelum.ini` with the repository's and take every value of his into `Luxvelum.ini`,
  keeping the repository's comments, before anything else; players keep their own backups of the file.
- Never launch the game client. Install without asking when the client is closed: stage `Logs\Luxvelum.log` and
  take the client as closed only when its last session ends with `game window closed`, which Luxvelum logs when the
  game window it is attached to closes; otherwise, the game still open or crashed, ask the owner first. Whether the
  bridge reports the DLL or the log open is no reliable sign either way.
  Check that his `WTF\Luxvelum.ini` holds the repository's values (else take his first, as above), then replace
  `Luxvelum.dll` next to `Wow.exe` (and `version.dll` where the owner's client uses it), `WTF\Luxvelum.ini`,
  `Data\Luxvelum\fogdata.bin`, `Data\Luxvelum\fogdata-without-darker-nights.bin`, `Data\Luxvelum\waterdata.bin`
  and `Data\Luxvelum\zones.ini`. While there is no
  `WTF\Luxvelum.ini` yet and a `Luxvelum.ini`, `Luxveil.ini` or `CoAVolFog.ini` lies next to `Wow.exe`, leave
  `WTF\Luxvelum.ini` out so the DLL takes the owner's settings over. Never replace `WTF\Luxvelum-zones.ini`: it holds
  the player's own zone halos. After an owner test, read `Logs\Luxvelum.log` in the client folder.
- `data/zones.ini` holds the owner's zone values (sun and moon halos, fog density), the defaults every release ships.
  Whenever you install into his client or prepare a release, fold the entries of his `WTF\Luxvelum-zones.ini` into
  it with `tools/fold_zone_halos.py` (his values win), then install the new `zones.ini` and empty his file, as he
  asked on 2026-11-25, so the shipped values apply to him.
- Keep plans and results in the conversation. Routine edits need no backups, receipts or standalone reports; use Git.

  `patch-A` to `patch-Y`). RTX 4060 behind DXVK 3.0, which reports AMD (vendor `0x1002`), so the RESZ depth path

## Repository layout

- `proxy/` — the optional `version.dll` loader that forwards to the system copy.
- `src/` — `d3d9_wrap` (device wrapper, INTZ depth), `msaa_depth` (multisampled depth copy into INTZ: NVAPI, RESZ,
  self-test), `dxvk_vendor` (`DxvkAmdVendor`: DXVK asked through `DXVK_CONFIG` to report AMD, so it offers RESZ),
  `hooks` (call-site thunks), `engine` (client addresses and frame inputs), `engine_actors` (unit walk for water
  contacts), `engine_lights` (point-light table walk, view-frustum test, ranked candidate pool, twin lights merged),
  `local_light_stabiliser` (eight slots with hysteresis, fades and holds across captures), `client_code` (client code
  views and byte guards), `client_light_stay` (the in-memory lightstay byte), `client_map` (the map id the far clip
  setter reads, the loading screen's as fallback), `client_camera_focus` (the position of the unit the camera follows,
  the camera position as fallback), `fog_data` (Classic fog data), `fog_data_choice` (the `fogdata*.bin` whose band
  probes match the client's loaded `LightIntBand`), `client_light_bands` (that table's records in the client),
  `fog_model` (layer parameters), `renderer` (passes), `light_on_screen` (the sun's or moon's position on screen),
  `celestial_occlusion` (the smoothed share of the sun's or moon's sight line that the world hides, with the farthest
  occluder), `character_on_screen` (the own character's cylinder around the camera focus and its box on screen, kept out
  of the god rays), `fullscreen_triangle` (the triangle every full-screen pass draws, its edges outside the viewport),
  `transparent_fog` (linear stock fog fitted to the volumetric fog for see-through models after the early composite),
  `overlay` (ImGui settings window: input chaining, `OverlayKey` and `ToggleKey`, drawing at `Present`),
  `settings_panel` (its widgets), `luxvelum_switch` (the off and on request of `ToggleKey` and the window, taken once
  per world frame by the hooks), `config`, `install_paths` (the client folder's `Logs`, `WTF` and `Data\Luxvelum`, the
  DLL's folder as fallback), `zone_halo` (the sun and moon halo and fog density factors per client zone, shipped in
  `Data\Luxvelum\zones.ini` and the player's own in `WTF\Luxvelum-zones.ini`, faded across zone borders), `log`,
  `status_log`; water: `water_data` (Forever presets, FFT tiles, foam masks), `water_classify` (liquid classes),
  `water_spectrum` and `water_fft` (GPU FFT waves), `water_contacts` (contact tracker, ring impulses at the client's
  ripple events), `water_ripples` (ripple map simulation), `client_ripple_sprites` (hold on the client's `waterRipples`
  splash and wake sprites), `water_renderer` (copies, stencil tagging, shading); look: `forever_look` (Forever glow byte
  before the fog composite, grading decisions at world done and frame end), `grading_renderer` (colour grading pass).
- `shaders/` — the ps_3_0 passes: `vf_*` fog (march, temporal, composite and its multisampled silhouette split, god
  rays and their late composite after an early one, depth probe and depth-copy check), `vw_*` water (FFT, ripple
  step, depth copy, shading) and `vp_grade` (colour grading). Among the includes, `vf_pixel_noise` holds the pixel
  noise of the step jitter and the display dither.
- `tests/harness.cpp` — the offline D3D9 harness; `--scene harbour` renders a logged in-game frame, `--scene ripples`
  units wading through the basin. `tests/shaders/` holds a copy of the client's M2 fog shader for the transparent fog
  checks.
- `tools/` — the Classic fog, client light and Forever water data converters and the comment check.
